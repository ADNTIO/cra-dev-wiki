# Professional help

This wiki is our technical work, freely available. If you'd rather do it with us — or have us do it for you — here is how we work.

## Three ways to work together

**Pre-audit and scoping.** First, figure out where you stand: does your product fall under the CRA, with which role and which risk class? Three days, a firm price, a scoping note as the deliverable — and what you need to decide on the next step, including without us.

**Article 14 compliance.** Everything you need to be operational by 11 September 2026: SBOM generated in your CI/CD, CVE auditing in the pipeline, OWASP Dependency-Track deployment, PSIRT procedures wired into the ENISA reporting chain. The episodes on this wiki give you an honest look at how we handle these topics.

**Monitoring and response.** Compliance doesn't stop the day everything is set up: fleet monitoring, SBOMs updated on every release, support for the 24 h / 72 h / 14 d notifications if the clock starts.

Details and pricing are on the [CRA Compliance](https://adnt.io/en/services/cra-audit/) page of our site.

## Where to start

The simplest move is to measure where you stand: the [CRA self-assessment](https://adnt.io/cra-quick-audit/?lang=en) checks five technical markers on your domain and qualifies your product in a few minutes. Free, immediate result.
